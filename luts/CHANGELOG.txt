Film Lab - Kodak looks (portra-160, portra-400, portra-800, gold-200, ultramax-400, ektar-100,
ektachrome-100, kodachrome-64, vision3-250d, vision3-500t .json)

Derived from spektrafilm by Andrea Volpato
https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm
Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (see SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt). Modified by Film Lab.

Changes: the spektrafilm profiles (spectral sensitivities, characteristic curves, dye and base
densities) of each film, and of the print stock it is printed on (Portra Endura paper, 2383 print
film), drive a simplified spectral model that is baked into a 40^3 3D LUT (input: F-Log2 scene
code, output: sRGB). Negatives print through their status-M densities; a simple inter-layer effect,
a grey balance and a light per-film colour cast are added. tri-x-400.json is not derived from
spektrafilm (hand-built tone model).
